At the conclusion of the freeze period, billing will automatically resume on the next scheduled billing date. Additional freezes are not permitted except where required by applicable law (such as military deployment or documented medical incapacity). This limited freeze policy is provided as a courtesy and may not be combined or extended.

❄️ 1 freeze per year · Maximum 30 consecutive days · Must be requested in writing before the billing date
